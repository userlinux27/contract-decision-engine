About Contract Decision Engine

What is this?

Contract Decision Engine helps small agency owners quickly understand whether a service agreement is safe to sign. Upload your contract and get a clear recommendation in under 60 seconds.

How it works

Your document is analyzed automatically. The system identifies potential issues and gives you a suggested decision with top three reasons. You also get safer wording suggestions for problematic clauses.

Privacy & Data

Documents are used only for analysis and are not stored permanently. Uploaded files are automatically deleted shortly after processing. We do not share your documents with third parties. We do not train AI models on your data.

Is this legal advice?

No. This service provides suggestions based on automated analysis. It does not replace a qualified lawyer. Always consult a legal professional for important decisions.

Current Status

This service is in Alpha (early testing phase). Features may change, and occasional errors are possible. Your feedback helps us improve.
