---

## 4. Kill Fee / Cancellation

If Client cancels after work begins:
- **Non-refundable deposit** retained by Freelancer
- **Work completed to date** billed at hourly rate $[HOURLY RATE]
- **Total owed** = deposit + (hours worked × hourly rate) - deposit already paid
- Client receives license to work completed only upon full payment

---

## 5. Intellectual Property

**Copyright:** Freelancer retains copyright until **full payment received**.

**Upon Full Payment:** Client receives full, exclusive, worldwide copyright in Deliverables.

**Pre-Existing Assets:** Freelancer retains all rights to pre-existing templates, frameworks, stock assets, and tools. Client receives license to use as incorporated in Deliverables.

**Stock/Third-Party Assets:** Client responsible for licensing any stock photos, fonts, or third-party assets specified by Client.

---

## 6. Portfolio & Attribution

Freelancer may:
- Display Deliverables in portfolio/website/social media
- Describe project in case studies (redacting confidential data)
- **Exception:** Client may request confidentiality for specific projects (must notify in writing)

---

## 7. Confidentiality

Both Parties keep confidential: business plans, customer data, trade secrets, unpublished work.
Survives **2 years** after project completion.

---

## 8. Termination

**By Client:** Kill fee applies (Section 4).

**By Freelancer:** 10 days written notice if Client:
- Fails to pay within 10 days of due date
- Fails to provide necessary materials/feedback
- Requests work outside scope without agreement

**Effect:** Freelancer delivers work-in-progress. Client pays for work completed.

---

## 9. Limitation of Liability

**CAP:** Freelancer's liability limited to **total fees paid by Client**.

**EXCLUDED:** No liability for indirect, consequential, or lost profit damages.

**EXCEPTIONS:** IP infringement, confidentiality breach, willful misconduct.
